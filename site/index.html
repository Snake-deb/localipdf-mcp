Free PDF tools for everyday documents

Compress, merge, split, sign and organize PDFs in your browser. Your documents stay on your device. No account needed.

Organize

  • Resize PDF Pages: Change paper size and fit your pages with optional margins.
  • Halve PDF pages: Split double-page scans into separate left/right or top/bottom pages.
  • Pages per sheet: Fit 2, 4, 6, 8, 9 or 16 PDF pages on each sheet for printing.
  • Merge PDF: Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.
  • Split PDF: Separate a PDF into pages, ranges, or smaller documents.
  • Organize PDF: Reorder, duplicate, rotate, and remove pages.
  • Remove pages: Select and delete unwanted pages.
  • Extract pages: Pull out exactly the pages you need into a new PDF.
  • Rotate PDF: Turn individual pages or your entire document around.
  • Crop PDF: Trim margins and crop pages visually.

Convert

  • PDF to PDF/A: Create a picture-based PDF/A-2b copy for archiving.
  • Rasterize PDF: Turn PDF pages into pictures inside a new PDF.
  • Extract Images: Save embedded pictures as separate image files.
  • RTF to PDF: Turn rich-text paragraphs into a readable PDF.
  • CSV to PDF: Turn comma-separated data into a readable PDF table.
  • PDF to SVG: Save PDF pages as SVG images, with editable text where possible.
  • HTML to PDF: Turn HTML text, headings, lists and simple tables into a PDF.
  • Markdown to PDF: Turn Markdown into a PDF with headings, lists and tables.
  • PDF to HTML: Create a webpage with PDF page pictures or selectable text.
  • Text to PDF: Turn pasted text or a text file into a neatly laid-out PDF.
  • Images to PDF: Turn JPG, PNG, and WebP images into a PDF.
  • PDF to images: Export pages as JPG, PNG, or WebP images.
  • PNG to PDF: Turn PNG screenshots and graphics into a PDF.
  • PDF to PNG: Save PDF pages as sharp PNG images.
  • Scan to PDF: Capture pages and turn straightened photos into a PDF.
  • PDF to Markdown: Save PDF text with headings, lists and simple tables as Markdown.
  • PDF to Text: Copy selectable text or save it as TXT, Markdown or JSON.
  • PDF to Word: Convert PDF pages to editable text or page pictures in Word.
  • Word to PDF: Turn a Word document into a PDF, with its pictures and tables.
  • PDF to Excel: Preview PDF tables and save them in an Excel file.
  • PDF to PowerPoint: Turn PDF pages into picture slides in PowerPoint.

Edit & Sign

  • Headers & Footers: Add page numbers, dates and text above and below your pages.
  • PDF Reader: Read, search and copy text from your PDF.
  • Annotate PDF: Add text, highlights, notes and drawings to PDF pages.
  • PDF Overlay: Add a letterhead, stamp or template PDF over another PDF.
  • Watermark PDF: Add your own text or image watermark to PDF pages.
  • Page numbers: Add page numbers to your PDF and choose where they appear.
  • PDF Bookmarks: Add and organize shortcuts to pages in your PDF.
  • Bates Numbering: Add unique page numbers that continue across your PDFs.
  • PDF Forms: Fill in a PDF form or create your own.
  • Edit PDF: Change text in simple PDFs, or cover old words and retype them.
  • Sign PDF: Draw, type or add a picture of your signature.
  • Edit PDF details: Change a PDF’s title, author and other details.

Security

  • Protect PDF: Add a password to protect your PDF.
  • Unlock PDF: Remove password protection from a PDF you can access.
  • Redact PDF: Permanently remove confidential content.
  • Remove PDF details: Remove saved details such as the title and author.
  • Flatten PDF: Make filled forms, comments and stamps part of the page.
  • Clean PDF: Remove comments, attachments, scripts and saved information.
