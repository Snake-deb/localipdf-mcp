Compress PDF to 200 KB help

Choose a PDF and try to fit it within 200 KB. The search keeps selectable text and checks page appearance. A result that cannot meet the limit is clearly marked.

How to use Compress PDF to 200 KB

  1. Add the PDF you need to reduce to 200 KB.
  2. Keep the preselected 200 KB target or enter a different size.
  3. Click Compress PDF to start the 200 KB search. You can cancel at any time.
  4. Check whether the 200 KB target was reached, compare the first page, and download your copy.
Options explained
Target size
Starts at 200 KB. Select a quick size or enter a positive custom value in KB or MB. Units use 1024 bytes per KB.
Remove saved document details
Optionally remove title and author properties; names printed on pages remain.
Use stronger compression
Works best with scanned PDFs and PDFs with images. Images may look blurry or lose detail. Check small print in the preview. Your target size is not guaranteed.
Limits and supported files

Unlocked PDF documents, including text and images. Signed files require confirmation to create a changed copy.

The requested size is an aim, not a guarantee. Large image files may stay above it even after compression. Stronger compression can make images noticeably softer; review the preview.

Tests covered 1,000 simple text pages and generated image PDFs up to 200 MB. Large files may use the full search time and remain above your target.

Tested in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. Large-file checks used a 100 KB target; the other starting sizes were checked with a 50-page PDF.

Questions

Can every PDF fit within 200 KB?

No. A long document or already compact file may not fit within 200 KB. The tool returns the smallest validated result it found and tells you when the target was not reached.

Will the 200 KB search turn text into a picture?

No. Selectable text is kept. Pictures may be resized, re-encoded or converted to grayscale; original files stay unchanged.

How long does a 200 KB search run?

At most 32 attempts within two minutes after analysis. It may finish earlier when no further search is useful.

How do I check a PDF reduced toward 200 KB?

Open the result preview to compare original and result side by side. Check small print and important values before using the downloaded file.
