Trim margins and crop pages visually.

Crop PDF help

Trim visible page margins by dragging the crop handles or entering measurements. Review your pages before saving.

How to use Crop PDF

  1. Choose a PDF and inspect the first page preview.
  2. Choose Apply to before changing margins. All pages is selected at first; choose Current page or Selected pages if needed.
  3. Drag the handles or enter the margins. Changes apply immediately. Open More options to find margins automatically.
  4. Select Save cropped PDF, then Download cropped PDF.

Questions

Does cropping permanently delete hidden content?

No. Cropping changes the visible page area; content outside that area can remain in the PDF. Use Redact PDF when you need confidential content permanently removed from the exported copy.

Can automatic trimming miss faint marks?

Yes. Faint writing, thin lines or textured backgrounds can affect whitespace detection. Review every suggested edge before saving, especially on scans, handwritten pages and documents with notes near a margin.

Can I crop a PDF by entering exact margins?

Yes. Enter the margins in Top (mm), Right (mm), Bottom (mm) and Left (mm). Cropping hides content outside the visible area without erasing it.

Can I crop only selected pages?

Yes. Choose Selected pages under Apply to before adjusting the margins. Review pages with different sizes.
