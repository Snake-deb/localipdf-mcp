Print-friendly PDF help

Turn selected pages into grey or black-and-white pictures, lighten paper and adjust contrast. Review faint writing and stamps carefully: cleaning can remove meaningful detail. This does not guarantee printer ink savings.

How to use Print-friendly PDF

  1. Choose a PDF and, optionally, the pages to prepare.
  2. Choose resolution, paper lightening and contrast.
  3. Optionally choose black and white only or reduce small specks.
  4. Click Make print-friendly and compare the result before downloading or printing.
Options explained
Lighten paper and shadows
Removes yellow tint through grey conversion and brightens estimated paper shading.
Contrast
Makes dark and light areas more or less distinct.
Black and white only
Replaces grey with black or white. Adjust the cutoff to keep the writing you need.
Reduce small specks
Removes isolated tiny marks. It may also remove punctuation or faint details; compare carefully.
Limits and supported files

Standard PDFs without switchable layers or XFA. Selected fillable pages need Flatten PDF first.

128 MB input/output/converted-picture working set. Rendering is bounded to 12 megapixels and 8,192 pixels per side.

Up to 500 source pages; prepare up to 100 per run, with a two-minute deadline.

Current browsers with local workers. Included in the initial offline cache.

Each PDF uses the same settings independently. Always review light writing and stamps.

Questions

Are the original words changed?

The way words look can change, and faint marks can disappear. Text on prepared pages is no longer selectable, and links and comments are no longer interactive there. Accessibility tags are removed from the new copy.

Does this perform OCR or compression?

No. It changes how the pages look. Use OCR PDF or Compress PDF separately.

Does print preparation remove hidden information?

No. Hidden objects and saved document details may remain. Use the appropriate security tool instead.

What about passwords and signatures?

Files that open without a password are unlocked locally. Opening-password files need Unlock PDF first. New signed copies require acknowledgement and have no valid original signature.
