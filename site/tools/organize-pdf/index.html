Organize PDF help

Rearrange a PDF visually using its page previews. Move, duplicate, rotate or remove pages, and add blank pages to prepare the document you want to share.

How to use Organize PDF

  1. Choose your PDF and select the page previews you want to change.
  2. Drag the selection into order, or enter Move to page and click Move. First and Last move it to an end of the document.
  3. Use Rotate, Duplicate, Remove or Insert a blank page as needed. Check the previews and use Undo to correct mistakes.
  4. Click Save organized PDF, then Download to save the page arrangement.
Options explained
Page selection and order
Select pages and drag them together, or use Move to page to position them accurately.
Rotate
Turn selected pages clockwise in quarter turns when scans face the wrong way.
Duplicate and remove
Make copies or discard selected pages; use Undo to restore an earlier arrangement.
Blank pages
Insert blank pages where a document needs a separator.
Keep bookmarks
Keep supported destinations attached to retained pages; duplicated targets use the first exported copy.
Blank page size, Size unit, Blank page width and Blank page height
Choose a standard page size or custom dimensions in millimeters, inches or points.
Blank page background
Choose the color of a newly inserted blank page.
Insert position and Reference page
Place a blank page before or after an existing page.
Blank page orientation
Choose portrait or landscape for an inserted blank page. This does not rotate existing pages.
Make form entries permanent
Shown when a PDF has interactive form fields. Keep the visible answers while making them uneditable in the new file. Digital signatures will no longer verify; keep the signed original.
Undo and Redo
Step backward or forward through workspace changes before saving. Your original PDF is not overwritten.
Jump to page and Thumbnail size
Use Go to find a page preview, or resize the previews for easier inspection. These controls do not change the saved page size.
All, None, Odd, Even and Select range
Select a group of page previews quickly. Enter page numbers in Select range and click Select, then check the highlighted pages before applying changes.
Move to page, First and Last
Move the selected group to a numbered position or either end of the document, keeping its internal order.
Blank page sensitivity
Adjust how much visible marking a page may contain before it is suggested as blank. Review suggestions before removing pages.
Limits and supported files

PDF documents and JPG, PNG or WebP images. Encrypted PDFs need their opening password when required.

Local tests used synthetic 10, 50, 100 and 200 MB PDFs containing large images. Chromium, Firefox and WebKit: completed 10, 50, 100, 200 MB.

Chromium, Firefox and WebKit: completed 50, 300, 1000 text pages.

Organize PDF was tested on a 16 GB Mac with Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. These are observed examples, not guaranteed limits on your device. WebKit tests do not replace a check in Safari on your own device.

Questions

Can I undo a removed page?

Yes. Use Undo in the workspace before exporting. The downloaded file includes your current page order, duplicates, rotations and blank pages. Your original PDF remains unchanged.

What happens to bookmarks and page links?

Supported bookmarks and internal links follow retained pages in their new order. Targets on removed pages are omitted. When a target page is duplicated, its first exported copy is used. A bookmark heading can remain when it still has retained children.

How do I move several pages together?

Select pages and drag them together, or use Move to page to position them accurately.

Can I fix sideways pages while reordering them?

Yes. Select the sideways pages and click Rotate. Each click turns the selection 90 degrees clockwise; use Undo if you turn too far.

How can I change PDF page order without dragging?

Select the pages, enter their new position in Move to page, then select Move. First and Last send the selection to either end. Check the new order in the previews before creating your PDF.
