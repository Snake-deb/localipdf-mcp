About localipdf

localipdf helps you work with PDFs without sending your documents to a processing server.

Why process documents on your device?

PDFs often contain personal or business information. When processing stays on your device, our servers do not receive the document contents. Once a tool’s files have downloaded, you can also use it without an internet connection.

What can I do here?

Use the tools to combine PDFs, split a document, rearrange pages, reduce file size or prepare an Excel or Word copy. Check the tool’s help for supported files and limits. Some conversions keep editable text; others create pictures of the pages. Choose the output you need and check it before sharing.

Who runs localipdf?

localipdf is run by Vick. For questions or support, contact support@localipdf.com.

How is it funded?

Vick funds the hosting and maintenance personally. The tools are free to use, with no account or subscription required.

Built with open-source software

localipdf uses open-source PDF, image and text-processing libraries. License notices are included with the app. Different PDF structures and browser limits can affect results; keep your original and check the copy before relying on it.

How can I check or report a problem?

The Verify it yourself page explains how to check document processing and network requests in your browser. If a result looks wrong, keep your original and contact support@localipdf.com with the tool name, browser and a description of what happened. Avoid sending private documents or passwords. A sample with invented information is usually safer for reproducing a problem.
